Hivegent is a self-hosted assistant that answers questions about your own documents.
Its agent searches, reads, and reasons over the files you upload, and cites the sources it used.

## What you can do

- Chat with your documents and get answers with source citations.
- Upload PDFs, Office files, images, and more, which Hivegent converts and indexes.
- Keep documents in a private workspace and in shared group workspaces.
- Let the agent work in several steps, search the web if enabled, and remember context across conversations.
- Connect your editor or other tools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

## How it differs from a plain chatbot

- Up to date: answers draw on your latest documents, not just training data.
- Grounded: every answer can point to the passage it came from.
- Agentic: the assistant picks tools and explores in several steps.
- Private: your documents stay on your own infrastructure.
