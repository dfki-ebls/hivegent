# Intended purpose

Hivegent assists authorised users with questions about documents they have uploaded to their own workspace or to a group workspace they may access.
It retrieves and cites passages, answers in natural language, runs the tools it is given to search, read, query, and compute over those documents, and writes results back into the workspace on request.

The system supports knowledge work.
Users must verify its outputs, and every mutation an agent performs is subject to the workspace access controls and, outside write mode, to explicit approval.

The system is not intended to profile natural persons or to make or materially influence decisions about employment, education, credit, insurance, public benefits, emergency services, law enforcement, migration, justice, elections, biometrics, or critical infrastructure.
It must not be used for prohibited practices or for any high-risk use listed in Annex III of the EU AI Act.

Hivegent is an AI system built on a third-party general-purpose AI model served by the operator.
It is not intended to train, modify, or place a general-purpose AI model on the market.
Because the operator develops it and puts it into service under its own name, the operator is its provider, which is what brings the transparency obligations in `article-50-2-operations.md` into scope.

Review this record before changing the system's tools, data sources, model, users, or operational purpose.
